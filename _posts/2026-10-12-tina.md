Tina is a Master's student in Molecular Life Sciences at Lübeck
University and has just joined our group as an intern. Like Marie
recently, Tina will carry out a short research project with us. Tina's
aim is to construct diagnostic genetic markers for the main
*Trichophyton* species from their whole genome sequences. The fungi of
the *Trichophyton* species cause skin infections in humans, including
such nuisances as athlete's foot. They are a rising public health
hazard, and there is corresponding interest in constructing diagnostic
markers for them. Let's see what Tina finds. Welcome Tina!
