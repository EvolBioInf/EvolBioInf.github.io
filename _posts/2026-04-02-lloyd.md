date:   2026-04-02 09:01:13 +2
---

Two weeks ago Lloyd defended his Master's thesis at the Institute of
Plant Sciences and Microbiology in Hamburg. Our host was
[Prof. Dominik
Begerow](https://www.biologie.uni-hamburg.de/forschung/organismische-botanik-und-mykologie/mitarbeiter/begerow-dominik.html),
who runs the [Institute of Organismic Botany and
Mycology](https://www.biologie.uni-hamburg.de/forschung/organismische-botanik-und-mykologie.html)
and also heads the beautiful [Botanical
Gardens](https://www.hamburg.com/visitors/explore/outdoors/loki-schmidt-gardens-22440)
that surround the Institute of Plant Sciences.

Lloyd had used [our software](https://academic.oup.com/bioinformaticsadvances/article/4/1/vbae113/7721998) to look for diagnostic markers in
six species of smut fungi. Smut fungi are a group of plant pathogens
that infect such important crops as maize and wheat. For all six
target species, Lloyd found candidate PCR markers with excellent *in
silico* sensitivity and specificity. The next step would be to test
these markers *in vitro* to determine their real-world sensitivity and
specificity. 

Lloyd is hoping to continue in science, for which we wish him all the
best.
