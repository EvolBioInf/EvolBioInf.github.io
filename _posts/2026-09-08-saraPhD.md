Last week Sara-Lena officially started her PhD with us as member of
the IMPRS Graduate School for Evolutionary Biology. She will be
working on "Phylogenetically correct taxonomy of genome sequences",
which aims at reclassifying bacterial genomes into phylogenetically
correct taxa below species level.

We look forward to continuining our collaboration with Sara!
