## Courses
We run two annual one-week courses, an introduction to sequence
analysis on the command line, *Bioinformatics for Evolutionary
Biologists*, and a course on Go programming, *Go Programming,
Biologists!* .

These courses usually start in February with the introduction to the
command line, and then the programming one in March.

## Blockpraktikum and Internships

We often host students who need to complete internships or projects in
    order to finish their degrees. If you are interested in working
    with us please contact us with your CV and your main area of
    interest (computer science and/or bioinformatics).
